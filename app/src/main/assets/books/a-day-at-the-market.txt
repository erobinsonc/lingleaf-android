On Saturday morning, Lucia woke up early. Her mother had a long list of things to buy, and Lucia wanted to help. They took two big bags and walked to the market in the center of town.

The market was already busy. People were talking, laughing and shouting prices. There were colorful fruits everywhere: yellow bananas, green apples, red strawberries and big orange mangoes. The air smelled of fresh bread and coffee.

"First, we need vegetables," said her mother. They stopped at a small stall. An old man with a friendly face was selling tomatoes, onions and potatoes.

"Good morning!" he said. "How can I help you today?"

"Two kilos of tomatoes and one kilo of onions, please," said Lucia. She was proud because she remembered everything.

"That's six dollars," said the man. Lucia gave him a ten-dollar bill, and he gave her four dollars back. Then he smiled and put a small apple in her hand. "A gift for my best customer," he said.

Next, they went to the bakery. The bread was still warm. Lucia's mother bought a big loaf and four sweet rolls for breakfast. Lucia could not wait to eat one.

Suddenly, it started to rain. Everyone ran under the roofs of the stalls. Lucia and her mother waited next to a woman who was selling flowers. The flowers were beautiful, and they smelled wonderful.

"Rain is good for my flowers," the woman said, "but it is bad for business!"

Lucia laughed. While they waited, her mother bought a small bunch of yellow flowers for the kitchen table.

After ten minutes, the rain stopped and the sun came out again. Lucia and her mother walked home slowly. The bags were heavy, but Lucia did not complain. She was eating her apple and thinking about the sweet rolls.

At home, she put the flowers in a glass of water. "Next Saturday," she said, "can I make the list?"

Her mother smiled. "Of course you can."
