Tom lives in a small house by the sea. He is ten years old. He has a dog. The dog is brown and white. Its name is Max.

Every morning, Tom and Max walk to the beach. The sand is warm. The water is blue. Tom likes the sound of the waves.

One day, Tom sees a boat on the sand. It is a small red boat. It is old, and it has a hole.

"Look, Max!" says Tom. "A boat! Can we fix it?"

Max barks. He is happy.

Tom runs home. He asks his grandfather for help. His grandfather is a fisherman. He knows everything about boats.

"Yes, we can fix it," says Grandfather. "But we need wood, paint, and a lot of time."

For two weeks, Tom and his grandfather work on the boat every afternoon. They cut the wood. They close the hole. They paint the boat red again. Max sleeps in the sun and watches them.

On Sunday, the boat is ready. It looks new. Tom gives it a name: "The Red Max."

Grandfather pushes the boat into the water. Tom sits in the boat. Max jumps in too. The boat moves slowly on the calm water.

"It works!" says Tom. He smiles.

"Of course it works," says Grandfather. "We made it together."

That night, Tom cannot sleep. He looks at the sea from his window. He thinks about tomorrow. Tomorrow, he wants to go fishing with Grandfather in the little red boat.
